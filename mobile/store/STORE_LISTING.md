## 3. English listing

**Subtitle (iOS)**
Exchange & summer school prep

**Short description (Google Play)**
From acceptance to departure: a checklist and real language requirements for your host

**Description**
```
Accepted to an exchange or summer school? Gyohwan-gaja turns everything you need to do before you fly into one checklist, built for your host university and your dates.

• A checklist for your host: application, language scores, visa, insurance, housing, flights and course registration, in deadline order.
• Real language requirements: not a generic TOEFL/IELTS range, but each university's official rule, including Cambridge, CEFR, TOEIC, Duolingo, JLPT, HSK and local-language levels, with a link to the source.
• Visa and documents, step by step, with official embassy and university links.
• Deadline reminders 3 days and 1 day before, at 9 AM.
• Communities for students going to the same host.
• Mentors who already studied there: chat 1:1, then book an in-person or video session. Mentors verify their university email and pass a review of their transcript before they appear, and only students who finished a session can review them.

Covers 8,500+ universities in 97 countries.
```

## 5. App Store · 앱 개인정보 (App Privacy)

- 추적(Tracking)에 사용하나요? **아니요**
- 아래 항목 모두 **사용자와 연결됨(Linked to the user)** · 목적은 **앱 기능(App Functionality)**

| Apple 항목 | 비고 |
|---|---|
| Contact Info → Name | 가입 이름 |
| Contact Info → Email Address | 멘토 신청자의 학교 이메일 (선택) |
| Identifiers → User ID | 아이디 |
| Identifiers → Device ID | 푸시 알림 토큰 (알림을 켠 경우) |
| User Content → Other User Content | 게시글, 후기, 신고 내용, 수료 증빙 파일(심사 후 폐기) |
| User Content → Photos or Videos | 프로필 사진, 채팅으로 보낸 사진·동영상 (선택) |
| User Content → Other User Content (messages) | 커뮤니티 그룹 채팅, 멘토 1:1 채팅 |
| Other Data → Other Data Types | 본교·학과·학년·파견교·일정, 멘토링 신청(날짜·방식·지역·메모) |

**연령 등급:** 4+ (모든 항목 "없음"). 사용자 생성 콘텐츠가 있으므로 신고 기능(게시글 신고 → 관리자 대시보드)과 운영자 삭제 기능이 있음을 심사 메모에 적어 둠.

**수출 규정(암호화):** Info.plist에 `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` 설정됨 → 표준 HTTPS만 사용, 별도 서류 없음.

## 6. 심사 메모 (App Review / Play 앱 액세스) — 영어로 제출

```
Gyohwan-gaja helps Korean university students prepare for an exchange semester or summer school abroad.

Demo account (already set up with a fall 2027 exchange to UC Berkeley):
  Username: appreview
  Password: (see below)

How to review:
- Home: personalized checklist with deadlines. Tap any step for official links and guidance.
- The "어학 최소 점수" (language requirement) step shows the host university's official requirement with a link to the source.
- Community tab: "All boards" (전체 게시판) plus a board per host university. Every post can be reported (신고) and any writer can be hidden on the device (이 사람의 글 모두 숨기기); reports go to our moderation dashboard, where the operator deletes posts.
- Mentors tab: verified mentors (university email + manual transcript review) can be messaged 1:1. The three "Sample profiles" are clearly labeled examples and cannot be messaged.
- Mentoring sessions: a student requests a session (date, time, in person or video call) from a mentor's profile; the mentor accepts it in Me → My mentoring (내 멘토링). Sessions are real-time, one-to-one services between two people, delivered in person or over a video call outside the app.
  The app has no payments of any kind and sells nothing. Whether a mentoring session is paid or free is agreed between the two people in chat; the app only connects them and states that any arrangement and money between them is their own responsibility (Terms §4).
  Demo mentor account: Username appreview_mentor / Password (see below).
- Chats tab: a group chat per joined school community and 1:1 chats with mentors, stored on our server.
- Me tab → 회원 탈퇴 (Delete account): permanently deletes the account after a password check.
- Me tab → 알림 (Notifications): deadline reminders scheduled on the device, and push for mentor review results.
- The app is in Korean by default; Me → Language (언어) switches it to English.

The app needs an internet connection; it loads university data from our server.
Contact: gyohwan.info@gmail.com
```
비밀번호는 저장소에 적지 않아요. 제출 화면의 비밀번호 칸에 직접 입력하세요.
